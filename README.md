# Elevator Town — touch update

Open `index.html` in Safari or Chrome on a computer. The game still needs an internet connection to load its existing Three.js library and font. No installation or build step is needed.

For iPad or iPhone, serve this folder from a web host or a computer on the same Wi-Fi, then open its web address in Safari. This package is not yet published to a public web address. A local Files preview may not run the game.

## Play

- Tap the floor to walk, or use the round movement stick on touch screens. On a computer, use WASD or the arrow keys.
- Tap an elevator to reveal its large call buttons on the right. Press an arrow; it lights red. Your character walks to the doors and boards when the elevator arrives.
- Inside, tap a floor number. All locations use 1, 2, 3, 4. The available numbers depend on the elevator.
- Tap Out or tap the floor outside to leave the elevator after arrival.
- Tap a bed to sleep, a sofa/bench/chair/lounger to sit, or a blue water cup to take a sip. Tap the floor to stand up and walk away.
- The music button switches the gentle original music-box tune on or off. The speaker button controls game sound effects separately. Audio starts after a tap.
- The building button switches between following your character and seeing the whole elevator structure.
- Swipe to turn the camera; pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom.

## Changes

The three original locations are retained: hotel, parking garage, and shopping mall. This update adds interactive furniture, water cups, walking routes around obstacles, independent music controls, full elevator shafts with cabin ceilings, and large metal-style call and cabin panels. The nearby Ride prompt is removed. The extra bell button is now a door-open button, and the alarm uses a repeated buzzer sound.

## Verification

Browser checks covered map loading, calling and boarding an elevator, riding to the hotel bedroom floor, sleeping and waking, sitting on a bench, drinking water, and music toggling. Layouts were inspected at desktop, 390 × 844 phone, and 820 × 1180 tablet sizes. These are browser viewport checks; physical iPhone/iPad Safari testing remains recommended. JavaScript syntax was checked with Node.

Original game by Aiden Azarnoush. Original project README identifies the project as MIT licensed. The added music is synthesized from an original note sequence in the game; there are no downloaded music recordings.
