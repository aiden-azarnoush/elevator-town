About Azar Playground

I made this game for my son. He loves games like this, but none of the games we found had what he wanted, so we built our own.

My daughter (8) and my son (4) came up with the ideas, and they tested every version of my code. And here we are!

We will keep adding more games. It is one fixed price, with no ads and nothing else to buy. Enjoy!

Please leave us a review on the App Store. I read every one and will keep making the games better.
