# Natural hand gestures

`rps-natural-hands.png` is a transparent photographic sprite atlas generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this game. The same asset is bundled in the native app's `Web/assets` directory. The game clips each hand separately, then rotates and mirrors the sprites during the countdown and reveal.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production game sprite atlas. Create one wide image with exactly three isolated photographic right hands side by side, equally spaced in three equal-width columns. Left column: ROCK gesture, closed fist with thumb folded over fingers. Middle: PAPER gesture, open palm with all five fingers extended naturally together. Right: SCISSORS gesture, index and middle fingers extended in a V, other fingers curled with thumb holding them. Natural realistic human hands, warm medium-light skin, anatomical accuracy, subtle skin creases and fingernails, soft studio light, wrist cropped at bottom of each column, fingers pointing upwards. All hands same scale and same person, fully contained in own column with generous transparent margins. Genuinely transparent background. No text, no emojis, no outlines, no cartoon, no extra objects. This atlas will be displayed by clipping each third as a separate gesture.

## Yellow human hand edit

`rps-yellow-hands.png` was edited from the natural hand atlas with the built-in ImageGen tool. It keeps photographic anatomy and texture with deliberately fictional golden-yellow coloring.

Prompt: Use case: precise-object-edit. Production transparent game hand atlas. Preserve exactly the three photographic hand gestures, anatomical shapes, all finger positions, nails, creases, lighting, transparent background, crop and composition of the reference image. Change ONLY the skin color of all three hands to a warm golden yellow, a deliberately fictional inclusive yellow color like a polished yellow character, retaining realistic photographic skin texture and shading. Nails should also have yellow-toned highlights. Absolutely no natural beige, brown, pink human skin tones. No emoji, no outlines, no cartoon flattening, no text, no new objects. Keep three separate hands in original locations: fist rock left, open palm paper middle, V fingers scissors right. Full alpha transparency.

## Articulated robot hand atlas

`rps-robot-hands.png` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the computer opponent. Both new atlases are bundled in the offline app too.

Prompt: Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: production transparent game sprite atlas. One wide landscape image containing exactly THREE isolated realistic articulated robotic right hands, equally spaced in three equal-width columns, wrist cropped at the bottom, fingers pointing upward. Left: ROCK closed fist, thumb folded over four curled fingers. Middle: PAPER open palm all five fingers extended naturally. Right: SCISSORS index and middle fingers extended in a V, ring and little fingers curled, thumb holds them. Same lifelike anatomically proportioned robot design in all three, brushed silver metal finger plates, small rounded mechanical joints and subtle blue accent lights, dark flexible joint seals, realistic studio product photography and soft reflections. No human skin, no cartoon, no emoji. Each hand fully contained in its own third with generous margin; no overlap. Transparent background, no text or labels, no extra objects.
