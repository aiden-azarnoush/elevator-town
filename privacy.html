Azar Playground: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Azar Playground is a game for young children. We built it to be safe and private.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, or send any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.

What stays on the device

The app may remember small game settings, such as the look of the player character, on the device itself. This never leaves the device, and deleting the app removes it.

Internet

The app works fully offline. It does not open websites or links.

Children

Because we collect no data, we collect no data from children. The app follows Apple's Kids Category rules and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Contact

Questions? Leave a review on the App Store or open an issue at github.com/aiden-azarnoush/elevator-town.
